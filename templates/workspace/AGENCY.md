# Agency Operating Defaults

Use this file for public-safe agency, operator, or FDE conventions.

## Default Client Workflow

1. Read the client dashboard.
2. Read the relevant workstream README.
3. Identify the current objective, blocker, and expected artifact.
4. Use the appropriate skill.
5. Save outputs into the client folder.
6. Update state on wrap-up.

## Artifact Standards

- Reports should end with decisions, recommended actions, or explicit blockers.
- Plans should include owner, next action, approval status, and expected artifact.
- Drafts should identify source material and assumptions.
- Data exports should include source, date, mode, and whether live credentials were used.
- Approval previews should be clear enough for a human to accept, reject, or revise.

## Ecommerce Defaults

- Draft first, publish after approval.
- Keep product, collection, content, technical, operations, and reports as separate workstreams.
- Separate raw data from decisions.
- Preserve rollback notes for mutations.
- Treat SEO, merchandising, UX, and operations as connected.

## SEO Defaults

- Separate technical issues, keyword opportunities, content recommendations, and implementation notes.
- Mark fixture/demo/live data clearly.
- Prioritize recommendations by likely impact, effort, confidence, and dependency.

## Content Defaults

- Preserve brand voice and offer context.
- Do not publish without approval.
- Keep drafts, approvals, and final published references distinct.

## Lead Generation Defaults

- Keep prospects, research, draft messages, approval previews, and sent outreach separate.
- Do not send outreach without approval.
- Include personalization evidence before recommending outreach.

## Agency Defaults

- Every client should have a clear next action.
- Reports should end with decisions or actions, not just observations.
- Proposals should be specific to the client's current state.
- Outreach requires approval before sending.
- Never use private client data in public examples.

## FDE Defaults

- Prefer small, reversible changes.
- Maintain runbooks for recurring operations.
- Convert repeated one-off scripts into documented skills or runbook notes.
- Keep implementation notes close to the workstream they affect.
